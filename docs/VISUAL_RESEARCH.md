# Visual research

**The Last Groove: Zaad's Journey** · Narrative Systems  
**Yago Phellipe Matos Lopes**

## Direction

Zaad's journey begins in an inviting fantasy forest and turns into a decision to defend a threatened village. The visual direction supports that contrast: peaceful nature and soft lighting first, followed by tighter character framing and a more immediate sense of danger.

The Legend of Zelda series informed the project's sense of wonder, its relationship between nature and adventure, and the idea that danger can emerge from an apparently peaceful setting. The aim is to translate that tone into a small student project with its own scene composition.

## How the ideas come together

- **Setting:** simplified foliage, stylized terrain, and a forest/village composition.
- **Tone:** a calm introduction that makes the later threat meaningful.
- **Framing:** establishing views, closer character shots, and changes of perspective.
- **Pacing:** cinematic introduction, player interaction, another cinematic sequence, and combat.
- **Scope:** adapt the references to a feasible student prototype; no measured performance improvement is claimed.

This document condenses the author's original *Narrative Systems – Visual Research* submission at Saxion University. The course objective was to use visual research to guide a playable narrative experience. The links and reference moments come from that research.
